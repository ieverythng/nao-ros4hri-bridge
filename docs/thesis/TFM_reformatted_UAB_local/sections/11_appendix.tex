  start_planner_llm:=true \
  chatbot_planner_mode_enabled:=true \
  planner_llm_think:=false

\section{Field Dictionary}

The 24 machine-readable fields are grouped as follows:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Provenance and identity:} \nolinkurl{record_id}, \nolinkurl{source_file}, \nolinkurl{run_group}, \nolinkurl{backend}, \nolinkurl{model}, \nolinkurl{case_set}, \nolinkurl{case_name}, and \nolinkurl{category}.
  \item \textbf{Adjudication:} \nolinkurl{status}, \nolinkurl{expected_outcome}, \nolinkurl{reasons}, and \nolinkurl{attribution_class}.
  \item \textbf{Context and consistency:} \nolinkurl{all_required_context}, \nolinkurl{evidence_consistent}, and \nolinkurl{route_observed}.
  \item \textbf{Runtime observations:} \nolinkurl{planner_request_observed}, \nolinkurl{execution_feedback_observed}, \nolinkurl{clarification_observed}, \nolinkurl{failure_observed}, and \nolinkurl{replan_observed}.
  \item \textbf{Event counts:} \nolinkurl{fallback_total}, \nolinkurl{target_selection_count}, \nolinkurl{executed_skill_count}, and \nolinkurl{spoken_text_count}.
\end{itemize}

The source status codes are \emph{pass}, \emph{degraded}, \emph{fail}, and \emph{not scored}. The complete index below abbreviates them as P, D, F, and NS. Run groups are V34, ABL, HVL, HOL, and CUR. Model codes are QVL for Qwen3-VL, Q35 for Qwen3.5, G31 for \code{gemma4:31b-cloud}, G4 for \code{gemma4:cloud}, and NEM for \code{nemotron-3-super:cloud}. Attribution codes are SC for coherent stack success, MV for model/backend variance, HO for harness observability, and SR for stack contract/runtime.
