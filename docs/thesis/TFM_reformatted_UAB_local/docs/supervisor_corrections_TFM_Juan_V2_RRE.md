## Scope

This log records the corrections made in response to the annotations in `prism-uploads/TFM_Juan_V2_RRE.pdf`. Page numbers below refer to the annotated supervisor PDF, not the recompiled thesis. The source PDF contains 106 textual comments plus additional unlabelled figure highlights/markups.

The corrections follow the requested editorial rule: unclear or disproportionately technical material was simplified or removed rather than expanded unnecessarily.

## Structural outcome

- The title was shortened to **Design, Integration, and Validation of a Modular LLM-Based Interactive Architecture**.
- General robotics claims are now platform-independent. NAO is named only when the physical implementation or evaluated adapters are being discussed.
- The former standalone Runtime Contracts chapter was removed. Its essential data contracts are now part of the Implementation chapter.
- Chapter 3 is now one coherent **System Design** chapter containing requirements, principles, architecture, components, and runtime flow.
- The duplicated implementation-level runtime-flow explanation was removed. Curated implementation diagrams were preserved where their technical content remained useful, with only confusing, unsupported, or prospective elements removed.
- The validation methodology was rewritten around five clearly ordered profiles, a direct questionnaire, explicit pass conditions, and metrics whose 0--1 interpretation is stated.
- The compiled thesis retains the curated implementation explanations, diagrams, detailed acceptance criteria, and robustness set; the final page count is recorded in the verification section below.
