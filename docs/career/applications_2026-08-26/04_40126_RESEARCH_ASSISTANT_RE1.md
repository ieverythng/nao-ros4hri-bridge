Dear Members of the Recruitment Panel,

I am applying for the Research Assistant position in the Earth System Services
Group because the BIG-5 project requires the kind of interdisciplinary bridge
that has defined my recent work: transforming heterogeneous image and text data
into computational workflows whose inputs, model behaviour, and outputs can be
examined. BIG-5's study of how nature-related values form and spread across
virtual communities is especially compelling because it connects machine
learning with a substantive social-ecological question rather than treating
automated classification as an end in itself.

I recently completed an MSc in Modelling for Science and Engineering at the
Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, specialising in Data Science, after a BSc in
Mechanical Engineering. I completed my BSc under the Hungarian Government's
Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship, which covered my tuition and provided living
and accommodation support. I was later selected for CSIC's JAE Intro ICU 2025
research-introduction scholarship at IIIA-CSIC, where I developed my MSc thesis.
This progression has trained me to move between domain questions, data,
computational models, and research software.

At Aily Labs, I took responsibility for integration seams connecting
go-to-market data capabilities to SuperAgent, the company's general-purpose,
customer-facing LLM reasoning agent. I developed automated analyses that
produced validated CSV artifacts, aggregated summaries, and structured output
contracts for downstream orchestration. My work progressed from MCP server
tools to task-scoped agent skills and then into a governed semantic layer.
Curated Markdown resources described the meaning, relationships, permitted use,
and access constraints of approved SQL tables so agents could identify and use
the correct data without relying on raw schemas. I developed and maintained
capabilities used by Aily tenants including Sanofi.

When answer quality regressed or a data resource, skill, or workflow contract
changed, my team was responsible for evaluating and correcting the behaviour
from development through production. I traced failures across data
availability, table allowlists, semantic metadata, tool selection, DAG
payloads, and post-processing, then verified corrections before promotion.
Unit and integration tests, representative question sets, trace inspection,
continuous integration, and pre-production validation provided the release
evidence. This gave me practical experience with data organisation, Python
backend development, NLP-enabled systems, and the maintenance of research-like
model evaluations under production constraints.

My MSc thesis at IIIA-CSIC brought text and image processing into one embodied
system. I designed and validated a modular ROS 2 and ROS4HRI architecture for a
NAO robot that combines dialogue, language-model planning, symbolic knowledge,
deterministic execution, and feedback-driven recovery. On the visual side, I
trained a custom YOLO detector for a restricted object set and integrated it
with the robot camera stream. The grounding pipeline normalised detections,
applied confidence and label controls, maintained bounded object identities,
and converted accepted observations into provenance-bearing symbolic facts.
On the language side, structured dialogue and planner outputs were evaluated
against requested entities, execution traces, knowledge-base postconditions,
and controlled failure cases. This work taught me to treat model output as
evidence that must be compared with observable and human-defined criteria.

My earlier work provides a complementary scientific foundation. My BSc thesis
built and validated a neural network for turbulent-intensity prediction from
wind-profile data, requiring data preparation, model selection, validation,
and interpretation against a physical process. At ABS Consulting, I later
supported seismic qualification and structural assessment for UK nuclear-power
assets. The work required reviewable calculations, standards-based reasoning,
and technical reports in which assumptions and results remained traceable.

iTrader, my autonomous trading platform under staged development, has extended
my experience with data collection, model experimentation, and scientific
visualisation. Its input layer supports historical OHLCV backfilling and live
WebSocket ingestion, followed by solver-specific feature engineering and
validated task generation. The implemented offline stack uses PPO/GAE with
FiLM-conditioned policy and value networks, decomposed rewards, reproducible
run manifests, flattened JSONL and CSV metrics, and automatically generated
Matplotlib diagnostics. Building it has required careful dataset boundaries,
experiment comparison, failure handling, and visual communication of training
behaviour.

Watson, the personal agent platform I operate for software development and
research, adds practical system-administration and backend experience. Hermes
provides the agent harness and tool workflow, LiteLLM provides model routing and
an OpenAI-compatible proxy, llama.cpp serves a quantised Qwen model on the GPU,
and ZeroTier exposes the endpoint across my machines. I maintain its launch,
health, routing, recovery, and performance checks. This work has made Linux,
service configuration, logs, network boundaries, and reproducible model
interfaces part of my normal development practice.

My Data Science specialisation also covered the computational infrastructure
needed for large datasets. Parallel Programming included C, CUDA, OpenMP, MPI,
SLURM, GPU programming, and performance analysis. Distributed Systems covered
cluster and supercomputer infrastructure, batch workloads, distributed
databases, cloud systems, and fault tolerance. Through laboratory and project
work at UAB and IIIA-CSIC, I submitted and monitored cluster jobs, developed
parallel solutions, and assessed computational behaviour. I would bring this
foundation to BIG-5's high-performance data-processing requirements while
continuing to deepen it within BSC.

I could contribute immediately to Python data preparation, backend services,
NLP and image-model experimentation, system administration, and research
visualisation. My formal training is computational rather than in social
science, so I would approach the project's conceptual and qualitative methods
in close collaboration with its domain researchers. I am prepared to extend my
current software experience into the project's frontend requirements and to
learn the appropriate computational social-science and social-network-analysis
methods without treating them as interchangeable with general machine
learning.

Within BIG-5, I would be particularly interested in building
provenance-preserving datasets that connect image and text content to platform,
language, landscape, human coding, model version, and uncertainty. Comparing
automated assessments with human judgements, documenting sampling and
annotation decisions, and producing interpretable visualisations would allow
the computational work to remain accountable to the project's
social-ecological questions. The project's multilingual and cross-platform scope also
makes careful data governance, bias analysis, and reproducibility essential.

I work independently, document decisions carefully, and communicate directly
across technical and domain teams. I am fluent in English and Spanish, live in
Barcelona, and have studied or worked in Hungary, the United Kingdom, and
Spain. I would welcome the opportunity to support Dr. Johannes Langemeyer's
team and contribute to the Earth System Services Group's interdisciplinary
research.

Thank you for considering my application. I would be pleased to discuss how my
experience in data pipelines, language and vision systems, scientific machine
learning, and dependable research software could contribute to BIG-5.
