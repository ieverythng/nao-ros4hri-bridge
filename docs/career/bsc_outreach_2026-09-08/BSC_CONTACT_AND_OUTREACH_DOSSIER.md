## Confidence scale

- **Direct:** the person publicly advertised the vacancy, is named in it, or is
  the documented leader of the exact project or team.
- **Strong group-level:** the person leads the named team, but public evidence
  does not establish panel membership or ownership of the particular vacancy.
- **Adjacent:** the person works in the relevant institute or technical area,
  but no direct vacancy relationship is public.

No email address below has been inferred from a naming convention.

## Evidence limitations

The BSC vacancies were removed from the main careers site after their deadlines.
Role reconstruction therefore combines preserved local application materials,
BSC's LinkedIn vacancy copies, official BSC staff and group pages, EU project
records, and secondary advert mirrors where no official full copy remained.
Secondary mirrors establish role wording only. They are not used to establish
personal contact details.

The contact recommendations identify likely technical recipients, not members
of a recruitment panel. Except where a person publicly advertised the exact
vacancy or was named in it, the dossier labels the relationship as group-level.
No private contact information, guessed address or scraped personal data is
included.
