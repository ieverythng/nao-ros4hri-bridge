# Priority BSC vacancy and contact research

Research date: 8 September 2026

Scope: `401_26_ES_ESS_RE1`, `383_26_AII_AIT_RE2`, and
`397_26_AII_S_RE2`.

## Method and confidence rules

The original BSC vacancy URLs no longer expose their contents to automated
retrieval. The vacancy descriptions below are therefore reconstructed from the
corresponding job pages published by the Barcelona Supercomputing Center on
LinkedIn, checked against official BSC organisation and staff pages, official
project sites, and the European Commission's CORDIS record where relevant.

No email address has been generated from an institutional naming convention.
Every address below appears explicitly on a public institutional or project
page. A `vacancy-linked` contact is named in the vacancy itself. An
`institute-level` or `technical-adjacent` contact has a verified organisational
connection, but no public source proves that the person belongs to the vacancy's
recruitment panel or manages that specific post.

Confidence labels:

- **High:** the vacancy explicitly names the person or reporting relationship.
- **Medium:** the person's official responsibility clearly covers the host
  institute, group, or technical area, but the vacancy does not name them.
- **Low:** technically relevant only. Low-confidence candidates are not
  recommended for first-wave outreach.

- **Project:** ERC project BIG-5, *Fostering Internet-based Values of the
  Environment*.
- **Closing date:** 28 August 2026.
- **Reporting line:** the vacancy explicitly says that the candidate would
  report to Dr Johannes Langemeyer.
- **Mission:** bridge social-ecological and computational research to examine
  how nature-related values are created in virtual communities and how digital
  nature values affect environmental stewardship.
- **Principal duties:** prepare data for human and computational assessment;
  develop machine-learning methods for automated image and text analysis; and
  provide programming support to the research team.
- **Principal requirements:** MSc in computational science or a related field;
  data gathering and organisation; system administration; backend and frontend

  German, and Portuguese were listed as valuable.
- **Employment:** full-time, 35 hours per week; open-ended contract tied to the
  project's scientific activity and budget; starting as soon as possible.

Sources: [BSC-published LinkedIn vacancy](https://es.linkedin.com/jobs/view/research-assistant-re1-at-barcelona-supercomputing-center-4459705519),

[official BIG-5 team](https://big-5.eu/team/).

- **Public institutional email:** `johannes.langemeyer@uab.cat`. This is his

- **LinkedIn:** [Johannes Langemeyer](https://es.linkedin.com/in/johannes-langemeyer)

  adVancing AI-Enabled multidiscipLinarY eNvironmental pollution sciences*.

- **Mission:** contribute to multimodal foundation models for environmental

- **Principal duties:** contribute to EVELYN research, documentation, and
